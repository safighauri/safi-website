Safi Ghauri advises the founders and boards of digital-asset and frontier-technology companies, sitting inside a small number of them as General Counsel. He leads Esquare Legal, the Dubai-headquartered firm he founded in 2009, and is a Partner of Tahota Law Firm.

A barrister of Lincoln's Inn who trained at Clyde & Co, he is also a geopolitical analyst with more than 300 broadcast appearances and Pakistan TV's South America Correspondent.
