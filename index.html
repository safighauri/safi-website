Safi Ghauri is a Dubai-based General Counsel to digital-asset and frontier-technology companies, with 18 years in licensing, regulation, capital markets and disputes. He is Managing Partner of Esquare Legal, the UAE-licensed, crypto-native firm he founded in 2009, with teams in São Paulo, Islamabad and China, and a Partner of Tahota Law Firm (4,000+ lawyers, 30+ offices).

Called to the Bar of England & Wales at Lincoln's Inn and licensed to practise in the UAE, Pakistan and Brazil, he trained at Clyde & Co in London. His litigation record includes Pakistan's largest defamation award and a cryptocurrency matter won before the Supreme Court of India; on the transactional side, he acted for the Chinese investors in the USD 4.2 billion RUDA project.

In parallel he is a geopolitical analyst with over 300 broadcast appearances, Pakistan TV's South America Correspondent, and a speaker at Horasis, COP30 and the European Commission and UNIDROIT's DARTE series.
