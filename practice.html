Legal Practice

Safi Ghauri works as General Counsel to digital-asset and frontier-technology companies and, through Esquare Legal and the Tahota Law Firm partnership, advises across three active capital corridors: the UAE, Brazil and Greater China. The work sits where regulation is still being written, and where the answer to 'is this legal?' depends on which desk you are sitting at.

Fractional General Counsel
Heading the legal function · board-level counsel · fundraising and governance
+

For founders and boards that need a senior lawyer inside the business rather than outside counsel engaged matter by matter, Safi takes direct appointments as General Counsel, held on a fractional basis alongside Esquare Legal. He works with the CEO and board on regulation, entity structuring, fundraising, governance, IP and commercial agreements, and coordinates specialist counsel in each jurisdiction where the business operates.

He currently heads the legal function at InvoiceMate, Tharwa Finance, The Game Company, Klink Finance, M20 Chain and OpenSpace Africa, and was General Counsel to RWA Inc. from 2024 to 2026. Two of these companies are on the UAE Future 100 list.

Scope and fee structure are agreed per engagement after an initial conversation.

Fintech Regulatory Licensing
SECP · NBFC lending · BNPL · regulator engagement
+

Esquare's Pakistan team has acted on licensing for many of the country's digital-lending NBFCs, and has worked across Pakistan's digital financial regulation from the earliest SECP fintech frameworks to the formation of PVARA, advising the companies seeking licences and presenting to the regulators issuing them.

Blockchain Architecture & Smart Contract Law
Enforceability · RWA tokenization · institutional infrastructure
+

Smart contracts are not self-enforcing in any jurisdiction that matters commercially. The code executes; the legal effect of that execution is a separate question.

Safi has worked with projects on Ethereum, Stellar, Canton and Hedera, and advises companies on designing tokenization and smart-contract systems that hold up under enforcement scrutiny in every jurisdiction where their counterparties operate. He has been cited in peer-reviewed research on the enforceability of blockchain-based dispute resolution.

Pakistan — PVARA licensing

Under section 70 of the Virtual Assets Act, 2026 and PVARA advisory PVARA/ADV/001/2026, existing operators had to apply for an NOC by 5 September 2026; that date has passed. Licensing now runs in three steps under the Pakistan Virtual Asset Services Regulations, 2026 (S.R.O. 1419(I)/2026): NOC (Reg 6), incorporation and FMU registration, then the licence application (Reg 7).
