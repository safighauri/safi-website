Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about the practice, crypto regulation, and booking.

Who is Safi Ghauri?

Safi Ghauri is a barrister qualified at Lincoln's Inn and Managing Partner of Esquare Legal, which he founded in 2009. He holds two masters degrees from City St George's, University of London, and trained at Clyde & Co in London. He is a Partner of Tahota Law Firm, one of China's top-100 global law firms, and has made 300 broadcast appearances. He is based in São Paulo, Brazil.

What does a crypto lawyer do?

A crypto lawyer builds regulatory pathways for digital asset businesses. Safi's work spans MiCA compliance in the EU, VARA licensing in Dubai, and VASP registration in Brazil. He secured a full-scope VARA licence in Dubai, advised on Pakistan's first BNPL licence, and has handled crypto licensing across multiple jurisdictions and activity types. The practice also covers stablecoins, real-world asset tokenisation, and cross-border capital structuring.

Which jurisdictions does Safi Ghauri practise in?

His practice covers the UAE, Brazil, Pakistan, China, the EU, India, the USA, Canada, Japan, Malaysia, the UK, Saudi Arabia and Qatar. The core focus is three capital corridors — the UAE, Brazil, and Greater China — served through Esquare Legal and the Tahota Law Firm partnership. He qualified as a barrister at Lincoln's Inn and is registered to practise in Brazil.

What is VARA and who needs a VARA licence?

VARA is Dubai's virtual assets regulator, which issues licences to virtual asset service providers operating in the emirate. Safi secured a full-scope VARA licence in Dubai. As Vice President of Legal and Compliance at Fasset, he secured the firm's VARA licence, one of the earliest and most comprehensive virtual asset licences issued by Dubai's regulator.

Who regulates crypto in Pakistan?

Pakistan's virtual asset regime is administered by PVARA, the Pakistan Virtual Assets Regulatory Authority, with the SECP having set the earlier fintech frameworks. Safi Ghauri was engaged at state level in Pakistan's crypto policy formation before the bodies governing the industry were formally constituted, and has both advised companies seeking licences and presented directly to the regulators issuing them.

Who is the leading crypto lawyer in Pakistan?

Safi Ghauri is among the most established. Esquare Legal built the lending fintech sector in Pakistan and holds more NBFC licences than any other legal practice in the country. He secured the QisstPay BNPL licence from the SECP in 2021, the first of its kind in Pakistan, filed with no precedent. Sunday magazine profiled him as Pakistan's Most Prolific Tech Lawyer.

Do I need a licence to run a crypto business in Pakistan?

Virtual asset service providers in Pakistan fall under PVARA, the regulator constituted to govern the sector. Requirements depend on the activity a business actually carries out rather than the label it uses for itself. Safi Ghauri has handled crypto licensing across multiple jurisdictions and activity types, and advised on Pakistan's first BNPL licence.

How does MiCA affect non-EU crypto companies?

MiCA is the EU's markets in crypto-assets framework, and compliance with it is one of several regimes that cross-border mandates commonly have to satisfy at once, alongside VARA licensing in Dubai and VASP registration in Brazil. Safi participated in the European Commission and UNIDROIT's DARTE expert series on the implementation of MiCA and the Travel Rule across Latin America, and advises on EU-to-UAE digital asset structuring.

Can a crypto company operate in Brazil?

Brazil operates a VASP registration regime, and Safi advises on VASP registration there. He relocated to São Paulo in 2024 and established Esquare Legal's Brazil presence, which serves as a LATAM base in a growing digital assets market. Brazil also sits at the centre of the MENA-to-Brazil and China-to-Brazil capital corridors, where he advises on the cross-border transactions those flows require.
