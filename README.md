# Safi Ghauri — safighauri.com

Source for **[safighauri.com](https://www.safighauri.com/)**, the personal site of **Barrister Safi Ghauri** (Safiullah Ghauri), Founder and Managing Partner of [Esquare Legal](https://esquarelegal.com/), a Dubai-headquartered crypto-native law firm.

Safi Ghauri is a Dubai-based General Counsel and crypto lawyer to digital-asset and frontier-technology companies, advising on VARA (Dubai), PVARA (Pakistan), BCB/CVM (Brazil) and MiCA (EU) licensing, RWA tokenisation, and cross-border structuring. He also works from São Paulo and Islamabad, and is Pakistan TV's South America correspondent.
