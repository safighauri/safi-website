Crypto lawyer in Dubai for Brazilian companies

Safiullah (Safi) Ghauri · Barrister · Managing Partner, Esquare Legal · Dubai

Safiullah (Safi) Ghauri, who publishes as Safi Ghauri, is a Dubai-based Barrister (Bar of England & Wales, Lincoln's Inn), General Counsel to digital-asset companies and Managing Partner of Esquare Legal. He works from Dubai and also from São Paulo and Islamabad, and advises Brazilian crypto companies and founders who want to license, structure or expand in the UAE without losing coherence with Brazilian rules.

What Brazilian crypto companies ask for in Dubai

  • VARA (Dubai Virtual Assets Regulatory Authority) licensing for virtual asset service providers, including OTC desks, brokers, custody and exchange activities.
  • Free zone and company structuring in the UAE, including RAKDAO and Meydan, for Brazilian founders.
  • Fractional General Counsel: contracts, fundraising, governance and regulator engagement for crypto companies and fintechs.
  • Stablecoins, real-world asset (RWA) tokenization, token launches and digital-asset disputes.

How the Brazil side is handled

Esquare Legal opened its Brazil presence in São Paulo in 2024. Questions of Brazilian law, including Banco Central do Brasil and CVM requirements, are handled together with local partner firms where the matter requires it. Safi advises on UAE law and on international structuring, and is a foreign-law consultant (consultor em direito estrangeiro) in Brazil. This page is general information and not legal advice.

Track record

As Head of Legal & Compliance at Fasset he led the legal work behind one of Dubai's earliest VARA VASP licences, and he obtained Pakistan's first BNPL licence from the SECP (QisstPay, 2021). He is General Counsel to several digital-asset companies, is a member of the European Commission/UNIDROIT Digital Assets Roundtable of Experts (DARTE) and a UNODC master trainer on crypto crime.
