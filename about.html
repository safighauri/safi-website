About

Safi Ghauri is a Dubai-based General Counsel to digital-asset and frontier-technology companies, with 18 years in licensing, regulation, capital markets and disputes. He heads the legal function at InvoiceMate, Tharwa Finance and The Game Company, and represents the world's second-largest crypto exchange before Pakistan's Virtual Assets Regulatory Authority. Earlier, he built the legal and compliance base behind Fasset's VARA licence and Series A.

He is Managing Partner of Esquare Legal, the UAE-licensed, Dubai-headquartered crypto-native firm he founded in 2009, with teams in São Paulo, Islamabad and China, and a Partner of Tahota Law Firm. He is called to the Bar of England & Wales at Lincoln's Inn and licensed to practise in the UAE, Pakistan and Brazil.

Legal leadership

His General Counsel roles are direct appointments heading the legal function, held on a fractional basis alongside Esquare Legal.

InvoiceMate (General Counsel, 2025–present). AI and blockchain receivables tokenization. He designed the legal architecture for DeFa, InvoiceMate's on-chain invoice-financing platform: offering and subscription documents, custody and escrow, invoice-pooling instruments, KYC/KYI and AML, the UAE licensing roadmap, and fundraising. InvoiceMate is on the UAE Future 100 list.

Tharwa Finance (General Counsel, 2025–present). A Shariah-certified stablecoin and yield protocol that tokenizes a range of assets. He is responsible for regulatory positioning, entity structuring, governance and commercial agreements with DeFi venues, and led the fatwa process through to certification.

The Game Company (General Counsel, 2026–present). AI and blockchain cloud-gaming infrastructure that raised USD 7 million in seed funding at a USD 40 million valuation and is now raising its Series A. He covers regulation, cross-border structuring, fundraising and IP. The company is on the UAE Future 100 list.

Klink Finance (General Counsel, 2022–present). A Web3 rewards platform live in more than 140 countries. He ran the legal side of the $KLINK token launch and its Tier-1 listings, including Binance, and the work on US and EU (MiCA) expansion.

RWA Inc. (General Counsel, 2024–2026). A multi-asset tokenization platform and token launchpad. He covered the USD 300 million tokenization mandate for Chinese new-energy mobility assets in 2026 (mandate value), the $RWA token and its KuCoin listing, and several token launches through the RWA Launchpad.

He is also General Counsel to M20 Chain (2023–present), and OpenSpace Africa, a Nigerian embedded-finance and lending platform.

Regulatory record

At Fasset (Head of Legal & Compliance, 2022) he secured one of Dubai's earliest VARA VASP licences, supported licensing in Indonesia, Malaysia and Bahrain, built the AML/KYC framework, and led the group restructuring ahead of the Series A.

At QisstPay (Head of Legal & Compliance, 2020–2024) he obtained Pakistan's first Buy Now, Pay Later licence from the SECP in 2021, with no precedent for the application, designed the digital-lending compliance framework, and led the pre-funding restructuring and investor documentation.

Through Esquare, he has worked across Pakistan's digital financial regulation from the earliest SECP fintech frameworks to the formation of PVARA, advising the companies seeking licences and presenting to the regulators issuing them. He currently represents the world's second-largest crypto exchange and several leading fintechs before PVARA.

Litigation

He was lead counsel for the Jang/Geo Group and Editor-in-Chief Mir Shakil-ur-Rahman in defamation proceedings against Bol TV's Tayyab Baloch, which produced Pakistan's largest defamation award: PKR 100 million, with an apology, awarded by the Additional District and Sessions Court, Islamabad, in 2022. He represented all associations of Pakistan International Airlines, together more than 300,000 employees, in the PIA privatisation challenge, and has appeared in constitutional matters before the Supreme Court of Pakistan.

For a blockchain-sector client, he led a cryptocurrency matter before the Supreme Court of India, heard by three of the court's senior-most judges, directing the strategy and instructing Indian counsel through to a successful outcome. His LLM thesis was cited in the NRO judgment on the recovery of illegal proceeds, work that now informs his practice in crypto-asset tracing and recovery.

Cross-border transactions

As a Partner of Tahota Law Firm, which has more than 4,000 lawyers and 30 offices and is headquartered in Chengdu, he works on Chinese capital moving into MENA and Latin America. Esquare acted for the Chinese investors in the USD 4.2 billion RUDA project, recognised in China Business Law Journal's Deals of the Year 2024, and held the law firm mandate for the Chinese Embassy in Pakistan, the Chinese Overseas Association and the Chinese Business Association in Pakistan.

Brazil and Latin America

In 2024 he established Esquare Legal's Brazil presence in São Paulo. The practice serves two flows that few firms cover together: Chinese and MENA capital moving into Brazilian agriculture, infrastructure and energy, and digital-asset businesses entering one of the region's fastest-growing crypto markets under the BCB and CVM framework.

The analyst

Alongside the legal practice runs a fifteen-year record as a political and legal analyst: more than 300 broadcast appearances, including as a regular contributor to Pakistan TV, the country's international channel, covering national security, South Asian strategic dynamics, the geopolitics of financial regulation and the Middle East. He completed the National Security Workshop at Pakistan's National Defense University and is Pakistan TV's appointed South America Correspondent, reporting from São Paulo. The analysis feeds directly into his advisory work on where regulatory and political risk is moving.

Speaking and policy

He has spoken on digital assets regulation at the Horasis Global Meeting, at COP30 in Belém on blockchain infrastructure for climate finance, and at the European Commission and UNIDROIT's DARTE expert series on MiCA and Travel Rule implementation across Latin America. He is a UNODC master trainer on crypto crime and terrorist financing for judges, prosecutors and officials, sat on a FATF digital-asset panel in 2023, and has spoken at Stellar Meridian, ETH Latam and KuCoin Future Fest.

Publications

He is lead author of "CBDC implementation in Pakistan" (Journal of Indian Studies, 2024) and was an expert interviewee in a peer-reviewed study on blockchain arbitration published through the Chinese Arbitration Research Institute (SSRN, 2025). His work has been cited in Tsinghua University PhD research, and he is the author of a book on advocacy and mooting.

Background

The son of a senior judge and career civil servant, he grew up across more than seven cities and several countries. He trained at Clyde & Co in London and at international commercial law firms in New York and Orlando before founding Esquare Legal. Sunday magazine profiled him as Pakistan's most prolific tech lawyer. He is a deep-sea diver, a rock music fan and a serious drinker of coffee, tea and yerba mate.

He speaks English, Urdu and Punjabi fluently, Portuguese at working level, and basic Chinese and German.
